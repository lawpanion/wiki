Who we are and why we built Law Companion

Studying law in Nigeria means working through large textbooks, long statutes and hundreds of cases, often with little time and few people to ask. Many students end up memorising points they do not fully understand.

Law Companion was built to change that. We explain the law the way a good teacher would: in clear, simple English, with the authority behind every point, so that you understand it and can use it in tests, exams and practice. Our aim is simple: to be the one study companion every Nigerian law student can rely on, from the first lecture to the last exam.

The AI Tutor

Questions and explanations

Ask a question in your own words and get a full, clear explanation in formal but simple English. The tutor names the relevant statute or case, explains how it applies, and then suggests two follow-up questions to help you go deeper.

Problem questions

For problem questions, the tutor answers in the IRAC method (Issue, Rule, Application, Conclusion), the way examiners expect.

Practice and exams

The Examiner sets practice questions on the course and topic you choose: multiple-choice, problem and essay questions, matched to your level.

Topic research

Need a paper? Tell the tutor your topic and goal. It plans the paper with you, writes it section by section in a scholarly style with OSCOLA footnotes and a reference list, and lets you ask for changes to any part. You can then share or print it.

Built on our own materials

The tutor learns from our courses, statutes and decided cases, and combines them with its wider knowledge of the law, so its answers are both accurate to what you are taught and broader than any single book.

Courses and lecture notes

Lecture notes and textbooks

Read lecture notes for the major law courses, written for Nigerian law students and updated regularly. The first chapter of every note is free to everyone; a plan unlocks the rest.

Audio, video and past questions

Some courses include audio and video lessons that play inside the app, and past questions you can practise with.

Study offline

Notes you open are saved on your phone automatically, so you can keep reading without internet while your plan is active.

Reading plan

A reading schedule on the home screen shows your current and next study session with a live countdown.

Legislation and decided cases

Legislation

The Constitution and other important statutes are hosted on Law Companion and are easy to read and search. The tutor can read them too, so its answers can point you to the exact section.

Decided cases

Browse landmark cases by course and topic, each with a short summary and why it matters, or search by case name or subject. New students can try the cases free for a limited time; an active plan keeps them open.

Law Dictionary and Maxim of the Day

Look up legal terms and Latin maxims explained in plain English, with pronunciation where it helps. Every day the home screen shows a new Maxim of the Day, and you can browse the last seven days.

Law Wiki and Magazine: the Law Companion Gazette

Read

Browse articles on contract, constitutional law, criminal law, tort, commercial law, jurisprudence, decided cases and legislation, written by students from across Nigeria.

Write

Students on an active plan can write for the Law Wiki, where other students can contribute improvements, or for the Magazine, which carries personal opinion pieces that only the author can change. Your name, institution and short bio appear under your article, and students with five or more gazetted articles earn the badge Senior Advocate of Law Students (SALS).

Reviewed before it is published

Every article is checked and then reviewed by our team. Only accepted articles are gazetted, and every change to a Law Wiki article is reviewed before it goes live.

Plans, credits and referral

Plans

A plan unlocks all the lecture notes. Choose the length that suits you, from one month to lifetime access. You can pay by card, bank transfer or pay with bank, and your plan renews automatically unless you cancel it.

AI credits

The AI tutor uses credits. New students get free credits to try it, paid plans include credits, and you can top up whenever you need more.

Refer and earn

Frequently asked questions

What is Law Companion?

Law Companion is a study platform for Nigerian law students. It brings law courses and lecture notes, the Constitution and major statutes, decided cases, a law dictionary, an AI tutor and student-written articles into one account that works on this website and in the mobile app.

Who is Law Companion for?

It is made for law students in Nigeria, from 100 level to 500 level. The AI tutor adjusts its explanations to the level you choose when you sign up.

Is it free to join?

Yes. Creating an account is free, and you can read the first chapter of every lecture note without paying. A paid plan unlocks all the lecture notes, and you can top up credits for AI use whenever you need them.

How does the AI Tutor answer my questions?

It explains the law in clear, formal English and points you to the authority for each point, such as the section of a statute or a leading case. It draws on our own course materials, statutes and decided cases, together with its wider knowledge of the law and the web. It can make mistakes, so always confirm important points in the original statute or law report.

Can I read my notes without internet?

Yes. Once you have opened a lecture note while your plan is active, the app saves it on your phone so you can read it offline until your plan ends.

Can I write articles?

Yes. Students on an active plan can write for the Law Wiki or the Magazine. Every article is checked and reviewed by our team before it is published, and your name, institution and short bio appear under it.

Can I earn by inviting friends?

Yes. Every student has a personal referral code. When a friend signs up with your code and subscribes, you earn a commission, which you can use inside the app or withdraw when you reach the withdrawal amount.

Is my account safe?

You confirm your email when you sign up, and you can set a security question to recover a forgotten password. You can change your password in your account settings and delete your account at any time.

Is Law Companion connected to the government or a university?

No. Law Companion is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government body, court, university or examination board.

Is this legal advice?

No. Everything on Law Companion is for education only. If you have a legal problem, please speak to a qualified lawyer.
